 Web Version: Mobile Version Custom Booking Flow – Functional Requirements The booking flow itself will be in German for the customer . The designs we have already created for desktop and mobile should be implemented as closely as possible to the provided mockups . We spent a lot of time refining the UI, so please do not redesign or reinterpret the booking interface. This specification mainly explains the functionality, pricing logic and booking behavior behind the existing design . 1. Booking Flow The customer should go through the following steps: Select travelers and number of rooms Select number of nights Select arrival date Optionally select a room category Select an offer Optionally upgrade meal plan / cancellation conditions Continue to booking The selections in each step affect the availability and prices shown in the following steps. 2. Travelers &amp; Number of Rooms The customer first selects: Number of adults Number of children Age of each child Number of rooms Example : Room 1 2 adults 1 child Child age : 4 Room 2 2 adults 2 children Child ages : 2 and 8 The number and ages of travelers must be taken into account when calculating all prices shown later in the booking flow. 3. Number of Nights After selecting the travelers, the customer selects the length of stay. For example : 2 nights 3 nights 4 nights 5 nights 6 nights 7 nights Each duration already displays a starting price, for example: 2 Nächte ab 198 € p.P . 3 Nächte ab 285 € p.P . These prices must not be static . As soon as the traveler configuration changes, the prices displayed for the different stay durations must be recalculated. The displayed price should be the average price per person for that configuration. 4. Calendar &amp; Automatic Departure Date Once the customer has selected the number of nights, they only need to select the arrival date in the calendar. The departure date must be calculated automatically. Example: Selected duration: 3 nights Customer selects: 10 September 2026 The system automatically sets: Arrival: 10 September 2026 Departure: 13 September 2026 The customer should not have to manually select the departure date . In the UI: Arrival date = clearly highlighted Departure date = clearly highlighted Dates between arrival and departure = light blue This should follow the calendar design from our final mockup. 5. Calendar Prices Prices shown inside the calendar must dynamically reflect: Number of adults Number of children Ages of children Number of rooms Selected number of nights Room availability Applicable pricing rules The displayed calendar price should represent the average price per person for the complete selected stay , starting on that particular arrival date. So the price displayed on September 10 for a 3-night selection means: Average price per person for a stay from September 10 to September 13. It is not simply the price of the night of September 10 . 6. Base Pricing Is Entered Per Night In the backend/admin system, hotel prices are entered per night . Example: Validity: 01 September 2026 – 20 October 2026 Pricing: Sunday – Thursday: €100 per night Friday – Saturday: €120 per night The system then calculates the total automatically depending on the selected stay. Example: 3-night stay consisting of: Thursday: €100 Friday: €120 Saturday: €120 Base total: €340 The booking engine then applies occupancy rules, child discounts and any additional services. The admin should also be able to override prices for individual dates or specific date ranges. 7. Child Pricing Child pricing must be flexible. Example configuration : 0 – 1.9 years Free 2 – 5.9 years 50% discount OR alternatively : Fixed price , e.g. €50 6 – 11.9 years 25% discount OR: Fixed price , e.g. €75 Therefore, the backend should support both: Percentage-based child pricing and Fixed child pricing Age ranges should ideally also be configurable. The child's exact age selected at the beginning of the booking process determines which rule applies. 8. Average Price Per Person Throughout the booking flow, we want to show the customer an easy-to-understand average price per person . At offer level, however, both values must be displayed: €458 p.P. and €916 total For example: 2 adults + 1 child Total booking price: €900 The system calculates the applicable prices for each traveler and then displays: Total price Average price per traveler The backend should remain the source of truth for the calculation. 9. Selection Must Be Stored in the URL The booking selection should be represented in the URL.
