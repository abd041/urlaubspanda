Prices must always be recalculated server-side. 10. Room Category Selection After the date selection, the customer sees the room categories. Please follow the provided room-category design closely. On mobile, this should be a horizontal slider/carousel . Approximately 2.5 room cards should be visible at the same time so that the customer immediately understands that more rooms can be viewed by swiping horizontally. Example : Doppelzimmer Balkon 2 Personen · 24–28 m² Unsere Empfehlung ab 458 € p.P . 916 € pro Zimmer The selected room category receives the blue selection state/checkmark shown in the mockup. 11. Room Category Selection Is Optional The customer does not have to select a room category. If no room category is selected: → Show offers from all available room categories . If the customer selects: Doppelzimmer Balkon → Only show offers matching that room category. This makes the room-category section effectively an optional filter. 12. Unavailable Room Categories A room category may be unavailable because of: Selected dates Inventory Number of adults Number of children Child ages Minimum occupancy Maximum occupancy Room configuration Do not simply remove such rooms from the interface. Keep the room card visible but display it in a disabled/grey state . Clearly show: Nicht verfügbar Optionally, where we know the reason: Für deine ausgewählte Belegung nicht verfügbar or Für diesen Zeitraum nicht verfügbar The unavailable card must not be selectable. 13. Room Details When the customer clicks: Zimmerdetails do not navigate to another page . Open the room details directly on top of the current booking page. Desktop: Modal / overlay Mobile: Large bottom sheet or full-screen overlay It can contain : Room name Image gallery Room size Maximum occupancy Bed configuration Balcony / terrace View Bathroom Air conditioning Wi-Fi Amenities Additional room information When the customer closes it, they should return to exactly the same position and booking state. 14. Meal Plan Upgrade Each offer has a base meal plan. Example : Verpflegung für 2 Personen ◉ Frühstück — inkl. ○ Frühstück &amp; Abendessen — +107 € The customer can optionally select the upgraded meal plan. The total price and average price per person must update immediately without a page reload . Different hotels/offers may support different meal plans, for example: Room only Breakfast Half Board Half Board Plus Full Board All Inclusive Ultra All Inclusive The system therefore must not hard-code specific meal-plan types. 16. Cancellation Upgrade Cancellation is another optional upgrade. Example : Flexibel bleiben ☐ Kostenlose Stornierung — +29 € If selected : Total price updates Price per person updates If free cancellation is already included: Kostenlose Stornierung — inkl . If no alternative cancellation option exists for the offer, do not display an upgrade option. 17. Offer Pricing Every offer should clearly show both the total and per-person price. Example : Preis für 2 Erwachsene 927,00 € 463,50 € p.P . For a family: Preis für 2 Erwachsene + 1 Kind 1.240,00 € 413,33 € p.P. The large amount should be the total booking price . The smaller amount underneath is the average price per person . 18. Multiple Rooms This part is particularly important. If the customer selected more than one room at the beginning, the system must guide them through the selection room by room . For example, if the customer selected 2 rooms: Room 1 of 2 Show the traveler allocation for Room 1. The customer selects : Room category Offer Meal plan Cancellation option The CTA should clearly say: 1. Zimmer auswählen After clicking it, Room 1 is saved and the interface continues to: Room 2 of 2 The customer then selects the configuration for the second room. CTA: 2. Zimmer auswählen Only after all rooms have been configured should the customer continue to the final booking summary. It must always be visually obvious which room is currently being configured. 19.
